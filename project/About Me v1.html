WHERE THE MONEY ACTUALLY LEAKS.

Six places revenue quietly disappears inside an HVAC shop. Same structure across all of them — what it looks like, what it costs, what fixing it looks like.

01

Missed Calls

Every unanswered call during a heat wave or cold snap is a job that went to a competitor. The fix isn't more CSRs — it's intelligent overflow, after-hours capture, and automated callback within minutes.

15–30% of inbound calls missed at peak. Recovery without a system: near zero.
02

Weak Follow-Up on Estimates

Tech quotes a replacement. Customer says they'll think about it. Then nothing — no structured follow-up, no nurture, no second touch. Disciplined sequences close the gap fast.

Industry ~30% close → disciplined ops 45–55%. That gap is pure margin.
03

Dispatch Inefficiencies

Techs sitting idle between jobs. Routes that zigzag across town. Senior techs running maintenance while juniors get sent to replacements. Nobody is tracking it, so nobody can see it.

1–2 billable hrs/tech/day lost to dispatch friction in most shops.
04

Technician Productivity

One of the least-tracked numbers in the industry, and one of the highest-leverage. Going from 5.5 → 6.5 billable hrs across a 10-tech crew is the equivalent of hiring two techs — without payroll.

+1 hr/tech/day × 10 techs ≈ 2 new techs, zero hires.
05

Review & Reputation Decay

Every happy customer who doesn't leave a review is a lead generator that didn't fire. Most shops ask inconsistently and lose ground to competitors who've systematized the ask.

Behavior-triggered asks lift capture 4–6× within 60 days.
06

Operational Visibility

Most owners run on gut feel and end-of-month QuickBooks. By the time you see a problem in the P&L, it's been bleeding 30–60 days. Real-time scorecards change how decisions get made.

Track: answer rate · close rate · billable hrs · review velocity · avg ticket.
04 · WHERE AI ACTUALLY HELPS

A multiplier — not
a strategy.

If your follow-up is broken, AI breaks it faster. Pointed at the right levers, it removes the human bottleneck on things that have to happen consistently or they don't work at all.

  • CALLSInbound call handling.Voice agents catch overflow and after-hours, qualify them, and book directly into dispatch. A safety net under your CSRs.
  • F/UPFollow-up automation.Sequenced outreach on every estimate, unbooked lead, and aging maintenance customer. The system doesn't forget — people do.
  • REVSReview capture.Triggered off job completion, personalized to customer and tech, with negative-feedback routing so issues never hit Google.
  • DASHOperational dashboards.The numbers that matter, surfaced — anomalies flagged before they become problems. Monday morning, you know exactly where you stand.
  • SOPSSOPs and training.Documentation that captures how your best techs and CSRs actually work, then trains everyone else against it.
