A FAMILY
SHOP STORY.

Before any of this was a method, it was just our family business — and a problem we had to solve. This is what happened inside the shop, in our own words.

01
CHAPTER ONE · WHERE WE STARTED

A shop that ran on grit.

We grew our family's HVAC business and pushed it past $15M a year. Trucks on the road every day, techs in the field, a CSR desk that never stopped ringing in the summer. Like most shops our size, the business ran on instincts, a tight crew, and a lot of late nights. It worked. But it ran us as much as we ran it.

02
CHAPTER TWO · THE THING WE FINALLY SAW

The leads weren't the problem.

For years we thought the answer to growth was more leads. Bigger marketing budget. Newer trucks. Then one summer we sat down and actually looked at the numbers — and the leads were already there. They were being missed during peak hours. Dropped between hand-offs. Quoted and never followed up on. The business was already busy enough to be twice its size.

03
CHAPTER THREE · WHAT WE ACTUALLY DID

We tightened the operation.

We put numbers on the wall. We rebuilt the way the CSR desk took calls. We changed how dispatch handed jobs to techs. We built a follow-up system that didn't depend on anyone remembering. And we started measuring the things that actually moved the business — answer rate, close rate, billable hours per tech, review velocity, average ticket — every single day.

04
CHAPTER FOUR · WHAT IT BECAME

The same demand, twice the revenue.

Nothing magic happened. We didn't spend more on ads. We didn't hire faster. The same leads, the same techs, the same town — they just stopped slipping through the cracks. Over the next few years the shop grew past $15M a year, and for the first time it ran without burning us out. That's the part we share with other operators now.
